## The job title

> **Senior Vice President of Apologizing to Geese**

A real-sounding corporate title with one hilariously specific twist: a senior
executive whose entire job is apologizing to geese. The geese, famously, do
not accept apologies.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the job title — and nothing else — to `/output/entry.txt`.
